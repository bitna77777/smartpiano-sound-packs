# Piano packs v1.1.0

Adds the first customer-download high-quality upgrade for Kawai Upright.

- `piano-kawai-freepats-stereo2-hq-1.0.0.spack`
- 66 true-stereo OGG samples
- 38 source pitches across two recorded velocity layers
- 44.1 kHz source, encoded as OGG Vorbis q6
- CC0-1.0 source: FreePats Upright Piano KW

The bundled mono, single-layer Kawai bank remains available offline as the fallback.
