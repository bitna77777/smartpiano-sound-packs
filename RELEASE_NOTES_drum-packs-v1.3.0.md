# Smart Piano drum kit expansion v1.3.0

This release publishes the optional acoustic drum kit upgrade for the drum screen.

- Virtuosity Acoustic Drums HQ: kick, snare, hi-hat, high tom, low tom,
  floor tom, crash, and ride
- Three recorded dynamics per piece with two alternates each (48 true-stereo
  OGG samples, 48 kHz)

The bundled Studio Drum Kit remains the offline default. The pack is immutable,
includes source/license metadata and per-sample SHA-256 checksums, and is
referenced by the detached ECDSA-signed production catalog.
